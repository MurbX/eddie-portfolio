( FAQ )

Good to know

Tell me what the site needs to do and who it's for. I'll suggest the simplest setup that does the job well.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the number of pages and how ready your content is. Landing pages move fastest. Your quote will include a clear timeline.

Do I need to sort out hosting and a domain?

No. I can set up the domain, hosting and business email for you, or work with what you already have.

Can I update the content myself?

Yes. If you'll be making regular changes, we'll plan an easy way to edit content. Otherwise, updates can be handled for you.

Can you also create the photos and copy?

That's the advantage of one studio: photography, video and graphics can be produced for the site, so everything looks consistent from day one.

Do you build online shops?

Simple catalogues with WhatsApp or booking-based ordering work well for small brands. Larger stores are scoped on request.
