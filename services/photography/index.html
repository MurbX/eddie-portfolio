( FAQ )

Good to know

Anything else? Send a message and I'll get back to you with a plan and a quote.

How do I book a shoot?

Send your date, location and what you need through the contact form or WhatsApp. You'll get a short plan and a quote, and the date is held once it's confirmed.

How soon will I receive my photos?

Edited galleries are usually delivered within about a week. For events and launches, a set of same-day or next-day highlights can be arranged for social.

Is retouching included?

Every delivered image is colour-graded. Key selects get detailed retouching, and extra retouching can be added for campaign or print work.

Do you travel?

Yes. I'm based in Kenya and available for shoots across the region and internationally. Travel is quoted per project.

Can I use the photos commercially?

Usage is agreed upfront in your quote, whether that's personal use, social media or full commercial and advertising rights.
