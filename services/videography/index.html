( FAQ )

Good to know

Every project is a little different. Send the brief and I'll come back with a plan, a timeline and a quote.

Do you handle the whole production?

Yes, from concept, shot list and filming through to editing, colour and sound. I'm also happy to work from your agency's script or storyboard.

What formats do I get?

Whatever your channels need: vertical 9:16 reels, 16:9 films, square cut-downs, short teasers and still frames for thumbnails.

How fast can you turn around an event recap?

Recaps can be delivered quickly when they're planned in advance. Let me know your posting schedule when you book.

What about music?

Films are cut to properly licensed tracks, or to music you provide and hold the rights to.

Can I get the raw footage?

Raw footage can be included on request. Mention it in your brief so it's covered in the quote.
