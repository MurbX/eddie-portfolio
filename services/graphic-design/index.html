( FAQ )

Good to know

Not sure what you need yet? Share where your brand is today and we'll work out the right starting point.

What do I get with a logo?

A primary logo plus the variations you'll actually use (icon, stacked, light and dark), a colour palette and type pairing, in print and web formats.

How many revisions are included?

Revision rounds are agreed upfront in your quote, so you always know what's included before we start.

Can you refresh an existing brand?

Yes. Sometimes a careful refresh beats a full rebrand. We'll look at what's working and keep the equity you've already built.

Do you handle printing?

All print work is supplied print-ready, and I can coordinate with your printer to make sure colours and finishes come out right.

Can you also shoot the product?

Absolutely. Design, product photography and launch video can all come from one studio, so the whole rollout looks consistent.
